Flood warning / watch selection

Flood maps are generated for one warning or watch product at a time. A single Flood Warning may contain multiple catchments; those detected catchments are grouped into the same warning where a product identifier is available.

Not scanned
Open the Flooding tab while connected to scan the current Flood Warning and Flood Watch layers.

The selected product controls the output extent. Both Flood Warning and Flood Watch overlays remain visible within that selected extent.

Map products

Exactly two products are generated for the selected warning type: Warning + Radar and Warning + Infrastructure Impacts.
